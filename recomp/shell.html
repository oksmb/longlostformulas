Point the page at your own copy of the game. Nothing is uploaded; the files stay in this browser tab.

To skip this step, put the CD's files (or game.iso) next to this page on a web server, or pass ?iso= or ?game= in the address. Streaming from another site needs that site to allow cross-origin range requests.

Arrow keys move, Space jumps, Alt attacks, F1 shows level information, M opens the menu, Enter confirms. A gamepad works too. Cutscenes are decoded in the browser the first time they play, which takes a few seconds each.
